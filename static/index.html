🔥 SmartMelt Studio Dashboard

SmartMelt Studio is a hybrid physics + machine-learning melt optimization suite for coreless induction furnaces, electric arc furnaces (EAF), and basic oxygen furnaces (BOF). This application replaces Streamlit with a high-fidelity client-side interface featuring real-time 3D Three.js furnace simulation and responsive interactive charting.

No physics calculations are duplicated: every API call hits the validated Python smartmelt backend, ensuring total numerical agreement with the mathematical models.

Active Calculation Modules

  • physics.py — Mass and energy ledger balances, dissolution kinetics, and implicit refractory heat conduction.
  • thermo.py — Wagner activities, partitions, thermodynamic energy floors.
  • ekf.py — Extended Kalman Filter for joint state estimation (efficiency and temperature tracking).
  • ml.py — Residual Gaussian Processes and XGBoost model headers.
  • chargemix.py — Least-cost blending optimization utilizing Simplex LP.
  • advisory.py — Bilingual metallurgical traffic-light advisory verdicts.

Core Physical Parameters (Verified v0.5 Literature Pass)

  • Latent heat of fusion (Fe): 247 kJ/kg (CRC Handbook 104th ed.)
  • Slag Reduction enthalpy (FeO + C): +1.39 MJ/kg FeO (Hess's Law consistent)
  • FeSi75 Heat of solution: -3,511 kJ/kg alloy (Sigworth & Elliott 1974)
  • Carburiser Heat of solution: +1,883 kJ/kg C (Graphite dissolution)
  • Grid emission factor: 0.712 tCO₂/MWh (CEA Database v21.0)
